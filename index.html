BJJ Kettlebell Plan

Two kettlebell days a week, one bell. Watch each figure to see the movement (tap it to pause), and tap a circle when you finish a set.

Progress

Warm up first: 5–10 minutes of easy movement, some hip and shoulder mobility, then two light sets of swings.

One bell does everything here. When a set feels easy, add reps or slow the lowering down before you reach for a heavier bell.

Pick a weight where the last 2–3 reps are hard but your form stays clean. Go lighter for the first two weeks, especially on get-ups and presses.

Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Swings and cleans are power work — end the set when the bell starts to slow down.

Keep lifting away from your hardest rolling days.
