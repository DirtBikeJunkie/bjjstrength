Warm up first: 5–10 minutes on the rowing machine or bike, plus some hip and shoulder mobility.

Pick a weight where the last 2–3 reps are hard but your form stays clean. Go lighter for the first two weeks.

Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Add a little weight or a rep once a set feels easy.
